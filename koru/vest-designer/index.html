02

The design

Start from a look, then move it, resize it, recolour it, or stack another on top of it. Fades, halftones, grain and ikat cover the whole panel and blend into whatever is under them, so two halftone screens in two colours behave like a print separation. It is all sublimated — the dye goes into the fibre rather than sitting on top of it, so nothing cracks or lifts.

Send it to Koru

We come back within two working days with a proof on the real garment, the cuts and sizes we'd run, and a date for your window. Nothing is charged until your runners order.

Designing here costs nothing and commits you to nothing. Cuts, sizes and the price your runners pay come back with your proof.

A new club vest design has to be registered with Athletics Ireland before it can be worn in competition. We'll send you the artwork sheet you need for that with your proof.

Every vest is sublimated, so colour is free: a six-colour halftone costs no more to make than a plain one, and nothing here is priced by the number of colours you use. Minimum runs and lead times are set by the mill and can move between seasons — we confirm them with your proof.
