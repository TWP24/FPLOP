How to read this

  • Both columns are forecasts — neither is actual points. xP is the model's raw output, which is what the optimiser ranks on. xP adj is the same number corrected for bias the model is known to have: measured over 8,392 player-months it runs about 6% hot and over-spreads at the top, so a defender projected high realises around 0.79 of it. xP adj is the one to believe for "what will this actually score". Real outcomes appear on the Charts tab, from gameweek 1 onward.
  • You cannot contest all ten months — eight chips across ten months means picking your battles. TARGET months are where the chips land.
  • ? marks a player with no Premier League history, whose role is inferred from price alone. Check these against team news before trusting them.
  • Chip values are low right now because the fixture list has no double or blank gameweeks in it yet. They appear from around December, and Bench Boost and Free Hit get much more valuable when they do. This page re-prices daily.
  • A two-gameweek month is close to a coin toss whatever your squad looks like. Spend chips where there are more gameweeks to work with.
